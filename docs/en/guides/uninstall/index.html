Removing the app completely

A normal uninstall takes the app away but leaves settings in place. If you want everything gone, here is the full order.

Windows

  1. Turn the tunnel off in the app and close it, otherwise some files stay locked.
  2. Settings → Apps → "ShtilVPN" → "Uninstall".
  3. Two settings folders remain. Open Explorer, paste %LOCALAPPDATA%\ShtilVPN into the address bar and delete the folder, then do the same with %APPDATA%\app.realityvpn.desktop.
  4. If the app came from Scoop, one command covers all of it: scoop uninstall -p shtil-vpn.

macOS

Through Homebrew a single command does everything: brew uninstall --zap --cask shtil-vpn — it removes the app together with its settings.

Installed from the disk image, drag the app to the Trash, then delete ~/Library/Application Support/ShtilVPN and the app's files in ~/Library/Caches and ~/Library/Preferences.

Android

A normal uninstall, same as any app: long-press the icon → "Uninstall". Settings go with it, and the system drops the VPN permission by itself.

What happens to your access

Removing the app neither cancels the subscription nor deletes your key — it stays with the bot. Install the app again, or open the key in another client, and everything keeps working until the paid period ends.
