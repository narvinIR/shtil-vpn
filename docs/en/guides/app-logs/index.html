What goes into the app log, and what does not

The app keeps a working log — it is what makes breakage explainable. Here is what is in it and who gets to see it.

What is written

  • Working events: core start and stop, settings applied, connectivity checks, errors with their text.
  • The app's own network calls — a reachability check, a request for the current version.
  • The core's log, in a separate file: it shows the addresses the core handles. That is effectively a list of the sites and services your device reached, so keep it in mind before sending the log to anyone.

Where it lives and how long

The log stays on the device, in the app's own folder, one file per day. Old files are cleaned up by the app so nothing piles up.

You can read it without leaving the app, on the Diagnostics screen.

Where it does not go

Nowhere. The app carries no analytics, no usage counters and no crash-reporting service — the log stays on your device until you decide to send it.

If you do write to support about a fault, the log shortens the work considerably: it shows the exact step where things stop.
