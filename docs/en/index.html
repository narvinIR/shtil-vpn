Russian sites bypass the tunnel

An ordinary VPN pushes every packet through the tunnel — and the bank starts treating you as a foreign visitor. Here the route forks on the device itself.

  • The lists of Russian domains and address ranges live inside the app and are never downloaded: a remote rule-set server can be unreachable from Russia, and then the VPN would refuse to start at all.
  • For domains on the direct route only IPv4 answers are returned — otherwise the device would follow an AAAA record past the route and the site still would not open.
  • Per-app routing: send only the apps you choose through the tunnel and leave the rest as they were.
  • Nothing is fetched at runtime: you switch it on and it works, even when third-party addresses are down.

Under the hood

The sing-box core

VLESS + Reality over TCP. The core is open source and ships inside the app — no internet is needed for the first launch.

A subscription link, not a long key

The configuration arrives ready and refreshes itself. When the key changes on the server, nothing has to be mailed out to people.

Over-the-air updates

The app finds a new version itself and offers to install it. On desktop only builds carrying our signature are installed.

Nothing extra

No accounts inside the app, no ads, no in-app purchases. The subscription and the payment live in the bot.

Questions

Do I need Google Play or the App Store?

No. The file is downloaded from here, and the app finds new versions on its own.

How do I install it on a TV with no browser?

Use a downloader app such as Downloader: type sub.ndvsdom54.ru/tv.apk with the remote and the file starts immediately. The address is deliberately short and has no “https://”.

Why do banking and government sites usually break under a VPN?

Because their traffic goes through the tunnel and the site sees a foreign address. Here those sites stay on the direct route, and the rule list travels inside the app.

Can I use a key from another provider?

Yes, if it is a subscription link or a key in VLESS format. The app is the entrance to a tunnel; the server itself is not included.

How many devices per key?

Two at the same time. One subscription link covers the phone, the TV and the computer.

Is there a build for iPhone or Linux?

Not of our own. The subscription link works in third-party clients — v2RayTun on iPhone, or any client built on the sing-box core.

What about logs and accounts?

There are no accounts inside the app, no ads and no in-app purchases. For the subscription the bot knows only your Telegram account and the paid-until date.
